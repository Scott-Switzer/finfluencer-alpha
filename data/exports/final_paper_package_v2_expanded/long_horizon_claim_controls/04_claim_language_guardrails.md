# Claim Language Guardrails

# Long-horizon claim controls

504D and very long windows are **diagnostic**, not standalone alpha claims. Thin effective sample (`n<80` baseline, stricter for 504D) forces **downgraded** language: cite only with overlap, censoring, and confound caveats.

Non-top 504D weakness is expected to be **noisier** than medium horizons; do not stack long-horizon non-top signals without manual data validation.
