# Results narrative (safe wording)

## Primary story

In **2,341** transcript-supported YouTube stock recommendations, there is **no defensible broad short-window abnormal return** or tradable finfluencer strategy. Returns are **heterogeneous**: mega-cap (top-5) names show **positive raw** short-window dynamics that **weaken** under factor adjustments, momentum controls, and placebos; **non-top** names show **weaker medium-horizon** performance that we **cannot** validate on a public-news-clean subsample (**non-top master-clean n = 0**).

## Mechanism (information environment)

The **information environment** pass asks whether YouTube adds signal beyond public Wall Street and market narratives:

- **Transcript relay language** (analyst/earnings/news/market-move keywords in evidence snippets) is associated with patterns consistent with **repackaging**, not incremental alpha.
- **Market sentiment regimes** (VIX, SPY/QQQ trend, drawdowns) **condition** heterogeneity — they are not causal proof.
- **Analyst alignment** is testable only when **dated** consensus exists; latest-only snapshots are **diagnostic only**.
- **Incremental predictive value:** transcript/creator features generally add **little** over market baselines for broad positive returns (holdout AUC ≈ **0.53–0.56**); language may help flag **non-top underperformance** in exploratory models — **not** tradability.

## Falsification anchors

| Finding | Safe wording |
| --- | --- |
| Cross-ticker placebo 5D | Treated minus placebo ≈ **+0.19%** — economically **near zero** |
| Market-quiet non-top 21D | ≈ **-0.56%** SPY BHAR — **sensitivity only**, not news-clean |
| Multiple testing | 57/73 survive BH FDR 10% — hierarchy controls interpretation |

## Do not write

- “Finfluencers generate alpha”
- “Results survive full public-news controls”
- “Analyst consensus proves causation”
- “High AUC implies investable strategy”

Describe **attention and ticker concentration**, not skill or causality.
