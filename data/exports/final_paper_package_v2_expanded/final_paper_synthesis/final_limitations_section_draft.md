# Limitations (draft)

## Limitations

- Unknown news coverage is never classified as clean.
- Multi-source clean is strict and currently empty.
- FNSPID improves pre-2024 history but does not replace live disclosure feeds.
- Bloomberg validation is included as an institutional mechanism layer, but it is not causal identification and does not create public-news-clean alpha, creator-skill, or tradability evidence.
- Bloomberg `Analyst_coverage` is blank, so analyst coverage counts are not included yet.
- Bloomberg short-interest coverage is partial and should be treated only as optional speculative-pressure evidence.
- Bloomberg News Heat and News Sentiment are news-flow proxies, not manual headline audits.
- yfinance snapshots are diagnostic only for analyst stance unless dated pre-event rows are proven.
