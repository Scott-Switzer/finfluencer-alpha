# Limitations (draft)

## Limitations

- Unknown news coverage is never classified as clean.
- Multi-source clean is strict and currently empty.
- FNSPID improves pre-2024 history but does not replace live disclosure feeds.
- Bloomberg validation is now included as an institutional mechanism layer, but it is not causal identification and does not create public-news-clean alpha, creator-skill, or tradability evidence.
- Bloomberg `TOT_ANALYST_REC` analyst coverage is now included for 2,326 / 2,341 events, but it is institutional-following context, not proof creators copied analysts.
- Bloomberg short-interest coverage is partial and should be treated only as optional speculative-pressure evidence.
- Bloomberg News Heat and News Sentiment are news-flow proxies, not manual headline audits.
- The extreme-event news audit is diagnostic for the largest return reactions only; it does not certify the full sample as news-clean, and candidate-clean extreme events are zero in the current run.
- yfinance snapshots are diagnostic only for analyst stance unless dated pre-event rows are proven.
