# Bloomberg validation section

Bloomberg was added after the main pipeline as an institutional validation and mechanism layer, not as a replacement for the transcript, return, SEC, or public-news confound pipeline. Its role is to check whether the paper's mechanism interpretation is consistent with professionally sourced market, analyst, estimate, liquidity, total-return, and news-flow proxies observed around the recommendation events.

The primary static Bloomberg workbook plus the supplemental analyst coverage workbook produced **861,811** long valid observations, **87,375** daily market panel rows, **13,937** weekly analyst/estimate panel rows, and event-level as-of matches for **2,341** accepted recommendation events. The derived files standardize Bloomberg tickers into the repo ticker format while preserving the original Bloomberg security string in the long panel.

At the event level, analyst consensus is available for **2,326 / 2,341** events, analyst coverage count (`TOT_ANALYST_REC`) is available for **2,326 / 2,341** events, analyst-estimate or price-target context is available for **2,332 / 2,341** events, Bloomberg news-flow proxy coverage is available for **2,328 / 2,341** events, liquidity proxy coverage is available for **2,328 / 2,341** events, total-return validation is available for **2,328 / 2,341** events, and short-interest proxy coverage is available for **1,064 / 2,341** events.

This strengthens the paper by adding institutionally sourced checks on analyst relay, public-information overlap, liquidity and transaction-cost controls, attention/news-flow proxy behavior, analyst coverage, and estimate-revision context. In particular, Bloomberg consensus, analyst coverage count, target-price, EPS, and sales fields provide a stronger benchmark for whether YouTube recommendations coincide with conventional analyst information, while News Heat and News Sentiment provide descriptive news-flow proxies rather than manual headline audits.

The Bloomberg layer does not identify causality, public-news-clean alpha, creator skill, or tradability. The supplemental analyst coverage workbook was accepted from `Sheet1` because cell `B3` equals `TOT_ANALYST_REC`; this field is institutional-following context, not proof creators copied analysts. Short-interest fields are only partially populated and should be framed as optional speculative-pressure evidence. News Heat and News Sentiment are Bloomberg news-flow proxies, not manual headline audits, and event-level matches are backward as-of joins rather than proof that the information caused either the recommendation or the return.
