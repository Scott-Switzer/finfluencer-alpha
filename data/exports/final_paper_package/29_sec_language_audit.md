# SEC-Clean Language Audit

## Objective
Tighten the language regarding "SEC-clean" events to avoid overclaiming news isolation. The term "SEC-clean" specifically denotes the absence of nearby official SEC filings (8-K, 10-Q, etc.) and does not imply the absence of all market-moving news (Bloomberg headlines, analyst upgrades, etc.).

## Language Standards
- Replace "no news confound" with "not explained by nearby official SEC filings".
- Replace "news controls" with "SEC-only robustness".
- Explicitly state: "does not cover Bloomberg headlines, analyst actions, earnings timestamps, press releases, or macro/sector news".
